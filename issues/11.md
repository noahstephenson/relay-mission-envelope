# 11 — [Michael] Bound model error and physical applicability

**Physics owner:** @IncorvaiaM1

**Goal:** determine where the reduced-order propulsion and battery model is physically credible, and bound its discrepancy only where evidence supports a bound.

**Starting point:** `physics/evidence-template.csv`, `docs/assumptions.md`, `config/baseline.json`, and the segment ledgers in `results/baseline.json`. The 600 W at 4 kg reference, mass exponent 1.5, segment factors, 3–5.5 kg domain, pack energy, and 1400 W bus limit are illustrative assumptions.

## Physics work

1. Check the power law against a physical baseline. Use rotor disk area, air density, thrust, and induced-power scaling to explain when a mass exponent near 3/2 is plausible. Identify the profile, parasite, climb, motor/ESC, and electrical losses that this scaling omits or absorbs. Keep shaft power, propulsion electrical input, and whole-bus power distinct.
2. Build a compact source table with source/version, operating state, rotor or vehicle geometry, mass/thrust range, measured quantity, electrical boundary, fit/check role, and uncertainty basis. Fit a coefficient only with compatible data. Where possible, reserve independent data for a check and report residuals by operating state; do not treat a fitted point as validation.
3. Assess reference power, exponent/efficiency, and climb, transit, descent, establish, and relay factors separately. State which factors have direct support and which remain scenario allowances. Hover agreement alone cannot validate a full sortie.
4. Define the admitted physical domain: gross mass, per-rotor thrust margin, continuous propulsion/bus demand, voltage and current limits, and pack available energy at the relevant discharge. Distinguish an actual hardware limit from the current assumed 5.5 kg and 1400 W analysis ceilings. If a source cannot establish one of these limits, mark it unsupported.
5. Provide source-specific discrepancy or parameter intervals only where justified. Record transfer assumptions when a source uses different geometry, air density, voltage, or flight state. Do not turn an arbitrary sweep into a confidence interval.

## Handback

- Populated `physics/evidence-template.csv` or a replacement table with traceable citations and fit/check labels.
- A concise model-domain note, discrepancy calculations, and a list of supported, extrapolated, and unsupported inputs for issue 12.
- Recommended corrections to the baseline only when evidence warrants them; preserve the original illustrative result for comparison.

**Done when:** a reviewer can trace every proposed physical bound to its source and measurement boundary, and can see which parts of the sortie remain unvalidated. No new flight campaign or rotor solver is required.
