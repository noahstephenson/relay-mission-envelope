## Physics work

1. Derive local sensitivities of energy margin and maximum energy-supported dwell to the dominant supported inputs: propulsion reference power, mass exponent or efficiency, segment factors, available pack energy/reserve, auxiliary demand, and route times. Include gross mass where a continuous family is justified. Check analytic or automatic derivatives against independent finite differences within the admitted domain.
2. Propagate issue 11's supported intervals through all six options using one shared physical parameter realization per comparison. Preserve relationships among quantities derived from the same measurement or pack; do not give each option an independent favorable power error. State and justify any monotonic endpoint shortcut. For coupled or nonmonotone inputs, search or bound the interior explicitly.
3. Apply every gate and Noah's declared selector to each admitted realization. Keep the fixed wall/link failures separate from energy and power uncertainty. Classify regions where an option passes for all supported realizations, passes for some, or passes for none; identify where the selected option changes and where evidence leaves the decision unresolved. A bounded region is not a probability.
4. Produce one readable two-parameter decision map or mass/service boundary figure. Mark the 900 s demand, fixed-gate failures, the baseline point, physical-domain edges, and any evidence-supported uncertainty region. Report the B2-S2 break-even and continuous-power boundaries with enough precision to explain the near-boundary result.
